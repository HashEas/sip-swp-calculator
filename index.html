Set Your Income Goal

How much monthly income do you want in retirement? Enter it in today's value — the calculator handles the rest.

Example: You want ₹1 lakh/month. That's your goal in today's money.
📈

Inflation Does the Math

₹1 lakh today won't buy the same things in 2050. At 7% inflation, you'll actually need ~₹5.1 lakh/month to maintain the same lifestyle.

Rule of thumb: Money roughly doubles every 10 years at 7% inflation.
🏦

Building the Corpus

To withdraw income for 25 years (that also grows with inflation!), you need a large corpus. The calculator finds exactly how much using SWP math.

For ₹1L goal: You'll need ~₹10.5 Cr by 2050 to sustain 20 years of inflation-adjusted withdrawals.
💰

Your Monthly SIP

Now the magic — how much to invest monthly? With annual step-up (SIP increases yearly like your salary), the starting amount is surprisingly achievable.

Result: Start at ~₹35K/month, increase 10%/year (capped at 3x). Total invested: ~₹2.4 Cr. Total withdrawn: ~₹25 Cr. That's ~10x!
💡

Key Concepts

SIP = Invest fixed amount monthly (accumulate)
SWP = Withdraw fixed amount monthly (spend)
Step-up = Increase SIP yearly (realistic)
Wealth Multiplier = Withdrawn / Invested (compounding power)

Pro tip: Start early! Even 3 extra years can mean 30-40% less monthly SIP needed.
